# Combat presentation

Hits rock the chassis, weapons recoil, and impacts combine bass and metallic audio. Settings include an impact-camera-shake toggle; the former reduced-motion and low-graphics options have been removed.

Decisive eliminations get a skippable 3.6-second cinematic: roof falls tumble down the skyscraper, while destroyed robots collapse on the roof. Draws and disconnect wins go directly to results. These animations do not alter server timing or match rules.

The rooftop overlooks a city 130 world units below, with lit windows, moving traffic, clouds, and an aircraft. Facing outward near an edge tilts the camera toward the streets.

Run `node scripts/presentation-browser-check.mjs` with the local game running to check edge framing, the final fall, cinematic expiry, and round reset. Screenshots are saved in `artifacts/`.
